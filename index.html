01 — About

A designer who thinks in systems, a PM who still opens Figma

Headshot — 1200×1500

Drop a portrait in here — 4:5 crop, ideally under 200 KB.

I studied Communications and New Media at NUS with a second major in Computer Science and a minor in Interactive Media Design — which is a roundabout way of saying I never wanted to pick between understanding people and understanding the system they're using.

In practice that's meant carrying work from both ends. At GovTech I turned ambiguous questions from seven organisations into requirements a team could build against, distilling 450+ responses from a 6,000-participant study into an MVP scope covering 70% of target users. At Shopee I ran competitor benchmarking and journey mapping to expand Buyer Self-Collection coverage from 30% to 40%, then owned UAT end to end so it actually shipped clean.

Outside internships I led a team of five designers at NUS Developer Group, built Figma design systems that cut turnaround time, and wrote the scraping pipeline and Leaflet map behind a travel tool at NUS Fintech Society. I like the parts of this job where research, design, and code stop being separate departments.
