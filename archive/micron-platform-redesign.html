Context

An internal platform used by more than 200 people daily had accumulated a decade of additions and no editing pass. People knew their own path through it and nothing else.

The problem

Internal tools have a captive audience, which removes the usual pressure to be good and adds a different one: any change is a change to how 200 people already do their job. The redesign had to be defensible to stakeholders and painless to adopt.

What I did

  1. 01

    Audited the existing platform

    Catalogued what people actually used against what the site offered, and where the two diverged.

  2. 02

    Pitched four proposals

    Put four distinct directions in front of stakeholders rather than one recommendation, so the trade-offs were argued explicitly.

  3. 03

    Designed the chosen direction in Figma

    Took the selected proposal through to a build-ready design.

  4. 04

    Wrote the training material

    Produced the documentation and walkthroughs that made the rollout stick, instead of leaving adoption to chance.
