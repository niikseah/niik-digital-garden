Brand Style Guide

A personal brand system built as a magazine — logo, palette, typography, and a name card reimagined as a wearable lanyard.

Designer

Team

Context

A personal brand style guide, themed as a magazine — built around the idea of a reader flipping through it rather than scrolling a spec sheet.

Logo

The two i's in my name give a symmetrical, distinctive foundation for the mark, standing in as eyes for creativity. The dots are set in Flame Pea, tying the logo directly to the idea the colour carries.

Colour scheme

  • Flame Pea (orange-red) — creativity and passion for design. As the dominant accent it draws attention without overpowering.
  • Observatory (green) — growth, personal and professional, complementing Flame Pea.
  • Outer Space (grey-black) — composure and maturity, grounding the brand in elegance and discipline.
  • Spring Wood (muted yellow-white) — clean and fresh, a soft neutral backdrop that lets the stronger colours stand out.

Typography

I wanted fonts that read clean and minimal while still holding their own. Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro carries the body: sleek, modern, clean-lined, and professional enough to match the ethos. Qanelas balances it — bold and modern, bringing weight to headers and key text, elegant but robust against the streamlined rest.

Name card

Traditional name cards felt outdated, but I still wanted a physical artefact. Repurposing the card as a lanyard makes it memorable and wearable, and taking cues from modern access cards, a QR code carries the digital half — portfolio and contact details — so the object stays uncluttered.
