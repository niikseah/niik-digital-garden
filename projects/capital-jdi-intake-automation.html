Startup Intake Automation

Context

Capital JDI ran startup applications through a six-stage process held together by inboxes, calendars, and follow-up. Every stage worked; the cost was that a small team spent most of its week moving applications between stages by hand.

The problem

Automating the wrong stage would have saved nothing. The first job was measuring where the effort actually went, then finding a stack the team could own without an engineer on staff.

What I did

  1. 01

    Mapped all six stages

    Documented the intake process end to end, including the informal steps that never appeared in any written version of it.

  2. 02

    Quantified the manual load

    Attributed effort by stage and found that 70–80% of team effort was going into work a tool could do.

  3. 03

    Compared 15+ SaaS options

    Evaluated tools against cost, integration surface, and — critically — whether a non-technical team could maintain them.

  4. 04

    Delivered a phased 8-week roadmap

    Sequenced the rollout on Zapier + Airtable + Calendly so the highest-effort stages were automated first and each phase shipped something usable.
